## Registry and evidence workflow

For each prospect retain: organization, official URL, source/review date, category, public contact route, research owner, relationship state, Canadian territory, permitted sales/referral activities, exact editions, software rights, safety/import responsibilities, support coverage, parts, training, lead time, warranty, demo/rental policies, insurance responsibilities, referral/commercial terms, media permissions, next action and evidence expiry. Unknown fields stay null/unknown.

States: researched → owner-approved-to-contact → contacted → response-under-review → terms-documented → approved-for-specific-scope. Declined and inactive are separate outcomes. Only an authorized reviewer moves a relationship to approved-for-specific-scope, based on retained written evidence. A reply, quotation or website link alone does not grant dealership or media rights.

Before sending, verify the current official contact route and ask the owner to approve the exact recipient, content and purpose. Do not use guessed addresses, scraped personal contacts or automated sequences. Store confidential replies outside GitHub. Review withdrawal/opt-out requests immediately and do not add contacts to marketing lists.
