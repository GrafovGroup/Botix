# Phase 4 — 90-day organic growth plan

Prepared 2026-10-23. This is an operating plan and authored content, not evidence of sent campaigns, accepted inquiries or revenue. Phase 3's image coverage and mailbox verification remain dependencies. No paid subscriptions or campaigns are launched.

## Starting evidence

The production site has 144 bilingual URLs. On October 23 Google processed the submitted sitemap and discovered 144 pages; Bing accepted the sitemap and was processing it. Discovered URLs are not indexed-page counts. Traffic, rankings, qualified leads, response times and revenue have no verified baseline yet. Preserve unknown values rather than entering zeros.

## Days 1–30: establish useful buyer paths

Publish the six original EN/FR guides after review and release checks. They expand existing routes: commercial robot selection, site assessment, event rental planning, humanoid feasibility, logistics integration discovery and industrial ownership costs. Each guide ends in a task-specific inquiry brief with category and intent retained. Keep the existing canonical/hreflang/sitemap structure; no city keyword clones or unsupported product expansion.

Priority themes: Canadian humanoid sourcing and supervised pilots; robot rentals for business events; commercial cleaning feasibility; warehouse material handoffs. Review Search Console weekly by locale, page and query theme. Record indexing exclusions and verify causes before changing content. Use aggregated data and restrict account access. No automatic page generation from search queries.

Milestones: six bilingual guides reviewed, 12 rendered guide placements checked, all 144 routes healthy, both sitemap submissions documented, mailbox receipt gate resolved, one accountable operator and retention policy recorded. These are work targets, not achieved metrics.

## Days 31–60: improve qualification and supplier evidence

Use real consented inquiries, if activation is approved, to identify recurring unanswered questions. Improve the relevant guide instead of publishing a thin new page. For cleaning, collect floor transitions, working windows, charging and access constraints; for logistics, collect handoff and exception requirements. Connect useful national/city planning content to those task guides without claiming local offices.

Research the five supplier prospects in SUPPLIER_PIPELINE.md. Owner approves each recipient and outreach purpose before sending. Obtain written Canadian scope, channel rights, support, training, parts, software and demo/rental terms. Record unanswered items as unknown. Do not publish a supplier logo, stock claim, pricing or relationship until its separate evidence and rights gates pass.

Milestones: every prospect has a research owner and next evidence action; response templates reviewed in both languages; actual qualification reasons consistently recorded. Outreach sent and responses received remain zero/unverified until real actions occur.

## Days 61–90: evaluate the evidence

Compare consented inquiry starts and durable accepted inquiries for the same reporting period. Review friction by language and task. Improve one documented problem at a time, then validate the affected page and inquiry route. Compare cohorts rather than inventing a conversion uplift. Keep a change log and note seasonality, small samples and incomplete attribution.

Review whether any new substantive content is justified by real buyer questions or verified supplier facts. Require unique licensed/original imagery for any new placement and bilingual parity before publication. Reassess search coverage, unresolved lead age, response coverage and supplier evidence. Decide the next quarter from actual observations.

## Measurement definitions

| Metric | Definition and source |
| --- | --- |
| Consented visits | Consented analytics observations only; current local custom events are not a deployed analytics collector |
| Inquiry starts | Explicit consent-gated form-start event; fixed locale/category only, no free text |
| Accepted inquiries | Deduplicated durable receiver receipts; distinct from local MTA acceptance and confirmed inbox delivery |
| Qualified rate | Human-qualified / accepted inquiries within the same dated cohort; exclude duplicate receipts |
| Source | Allowlisted direct, organic, referral, approved campaign or unknown; no raw URLs, query strings or full referrers |
| Response time | Accepted-to-first-human-response; report elapsed hours unless staffed business hours have been formally defined |
| Win rate | Won / decided opportunities in a defined cohort; undecided opportunities reported separately; no assumed revenue |

Use PHASE_4_LEAD_OPERATIONS.md for private CRM fields and qualification. Suppress small cohorts in external reports. Never send customer messages, names, email addresses, receipt IDs or site drawings to analytics. Optional analytics needs affirmative consent; operational records need restricted access and the approved retention/deletion procedure. No invented vendor IDs, cookies or tracking endpoint is added in Phase 4.
